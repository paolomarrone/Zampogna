# Guitar pickup and tone control

Lumped pickup model with coil resistance and inductance, cable capacitance, amplifier load, and a 250 kΩ tone control with 22 nF capacitor.

Entry point: guitar_pickup(x, tone).

References: [Acoustics and Modeling of Pickups](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/234034228_Acoustics_and_Modeling_of_Pickups), [guitar pickup equivalent circuits](https://macalisterelectronics.com/guitar-pickup-equivalent-circuits.html).

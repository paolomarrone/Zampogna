# Series RLC

One resonant circuit with low-pass, band-pass and high-pass outputs across C, R and L. Frequency and Q are normalized controls.

Entry point: series_rlc(x, frequency, q).

References: [wave digital elements](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Physical_Derivation_Wave_Digital.html), [general series adaptor](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/General_Series_Adaptor_Force.html).

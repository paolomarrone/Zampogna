# Mass–spring–damper

Force-driven mechanical WDF. Mass, spring and dashpot form the mechanical analogue of a series RLC circuit; the output is displacement.

Entry point: mass_spring_damper(force, frequency, damping).

References: [wave digital mass–spring oscillator](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Wave_Digital_Mass_Spring_Oscillator.html), [mass and dashpot in series](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Mass_Dashpot_Series.html).
